### Featured Projects

**HomeServices | React, Tailwind CSS**

- Finding a home service usually means jumping between scattered listings with no clear details, so I built one place where everything is easy to find.
- You can browse the services, check the details on simple cards, and pick what you need without any confusion.
- It's fully responsive, so it feels just as smooth on a phone as on a laptop.

**Himalayan Camping & Resort | React, Tailwind CSS**

- Camping and resort stays are often spread across different sites, which makes them hard to compare, so I put them together on one page.
- You can scroll through a card grid, see the important details at a glance, and compare your options easily.
- I used Playfair Display for the typography to give it a warm, scenic feel, and it works well on every screen size.
